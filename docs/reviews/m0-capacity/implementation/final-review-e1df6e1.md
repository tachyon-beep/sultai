## Issues

### Critical (Must Fix)

**C1. Extended admission fills to the hard ceiling with no reserve, on kernels that omit known costs, and nothing re-projects before the extended items run.**
`m0/costs.py:244` (`admit_extended`), `m0/costs.py:185-210` (`extended_cost`), `m0/pilot.py:251` (`admission`), `m0/pilot.py:669`/`:837` (`stage_extended`, called with no re-projection), `m0/receipts.py:274-290` (`derive_workloads`).
- *What.* `admit_extended` admits greedily while `projected + cost <= ceiling`, leaving zero headroom. Three costs the projection leaves out are visible in the code:
  - (a) `extended_cost` prices cooled-20, F(0.25) and F(0.75) as host plus `evaluation`. But `evaluation` is the in-process `mean_loss` time from `timing_action` (`worker.py:1087`, about 0.04 s), while `train_hosts` (`pilot.py:435-470`) launches three whole children per host: `train`, `noop-{s}` and `evaluate-{s}`. That is 12 uncosted child launches per item, each paying process start-up, dataset load and caching, plus the noop's R-bn and LBFGS fits.
  - (b) R-all is costed as 4 units of R-aff's per-1,000-step kernel (R9/R17). But `fit_retune` with `RALL_CHECKPOINTS` (`worker.py:124`) makes 20 host copies and 20 from-images selection evaluations per rate (100 per anchor, against 15 per R-aff grid), and `_retune_harm` evaluates all 100 candidates from images on the 5,000 development examples. None of that density is in the kernel.
  - (c) The `modules` and `retune` children's fixed per-child work (process start-up, `cached`, seen-fit features, cuts, leave-one-out, harm evaluations of every candidate) is not in the per-step grid kernels.
- *Why it matters.* The `Supervisor` turns an exhausted ceiling into `PilotHold` ("cap exhausted" or "aggregate GPU-hour timeout"), so `pilot()` publishes `execution-invalid`, `pilot_clean: false`, after `05-spreads.json` and before 06 and 07. The single custody attempt is consumed and the confirmatory refuses the result: four GPU-hours lost. On nominal §8 numbers the core (~8,750 s, plus a stage-1 overspend, see M2) leaves roughly 4,400 s. All seven extended items project to about 3,700 s, so all are admitted with about 700 s headroom, which the uncosted children above can plausibly exceed. §8 says extended arms run "only while the **measured charge** projects to finish under the ceiling". After stage 5 the measured charge exists and is not consulted. Core results are lost to an extended item, which §8's "core arms are never dropped" intends to prevent.
- *Fix.*
  1. Before each extended host item in `stage_extended` (and before the starved pair), re-project from `run.supervisor.charged` plus that item's cost. Stop at the first miss, record it in `05-extended.json`, and continue to stages 6 and 7.
  2. Cost the host items from stage-3 whole-child ledger charges (train + noop + evaluate of the same lineage kind), not `evaluation`.
  3. Cost R-all from its own terms (steps × kernel + 100 × measured from-images evaluation), or book it from a measured child.
  4. Optionally keep a small reserve in `admit_extended`.

  Also raise with John whether `06-confirmatory-count.json`/`07-development-medians.json` and a clean pilot result should be published before the extended host items run, since those items have no verdict role.

### Important (Should Fix)

**I1. Nothing binds the confirmatory's implementation source to the pilot's** (`m0/confirmatory.py:186-226` `admit_launch`; `m0/pilot.py:845-862` pilot-result).
- *What.* `pilot-result.json` carries the protocol identity but no `implementation_source_sha256`/`implementation_commit`. `admit_launch` checks only protocol identity. The confirmatory certificate is a new certificate checked against the code at launch time.
- *Why.* §10: "Any change to host, fit, slicing, re-tune or calibration code after the freeze is a new version with a new hash, a disposition and a fresh seed block." Committing the pilot evidence under `docs/` does not change `source_sha256` (it covers `src`, `scripts`, `tests` and `pyproject.toml`), so strict equality is achievable. Ledger Task 18 deferred this to gate C. A machine check is cheap, and a mixed-source confirmatory would be unreadable.
- *Fix.* Publish `implementation_source_sha256` and `implementation_commit` in `pilot-result.json`. In `admit_launch`, require `check_certificate(...)["source_sha256"]` to equal it, unless a hash-listed disposition in the certificate's evidence says otherwise.

**I2. The confirmatory places anchors on cards by list index, so with odd n a `retune` child runs on the other card from its own `modules`/`maff` children, and aggregation requires bitwise-equal N0 vectors across cards** (`m0/pilot.py:295-299` `Run.run`; `m0/confirmatory.py:292-306` `stage_core`; `m0/receipts.py:176-190` `anchor`).
- *What.* `Run.run` puts job i on card i % 2. In `confirmatory.stage_core` the list is `modules(n) + retune(n)`, so `retune-{s_j}` lands on card (n + j) % 2. For odd n (n = max(32, n*) can be odd, e.g. 39 at SD 0.03) that differs from `modules-{s_j}`/`maff-{s_j}` on card j % 2. `receipts.anchor` then demands `torch.equal` of the N0 loss and correctness vectors. In the pilot the parity happens to line up.
- *Why.* Cross-card bitwise equality of development evaluation is never established (the acceptance matrix compares training hashes and the grid rate across cards, not evaluation outputs). A mismatch is a `PilotHold` at aggregation after the whole confirmatory spend, which burns the block (R22). Only one restart block exists.
- *Fix.* Pin the card by anchor (e.g. `gpu = seeds.index(s) % 2` carried in the job), or have `Run.run` take explicit cards. Alternatively, add an acceptance item comparing development losses of one N0 across cards.

**I3. The confirmatory projection omits per-child overhead, and the stage-5 measured cross-check is optional** (`m0/pilot.py:698-705` `confirmatory_count`; `m0/costs.py:180`/`:278` `per_anchor`/`project_confirmatory`).
- *What.* The per-anchor confirmatory cost is host + noop-child + maff-child + tcool-child (whole children) plus grid kernels × steps for the `modules` and `retune` children, with no start-up, caching or harm-evaluation overhead. It is partly offset because `fixed_v0` is the pilot's whole stage 1–2 charge (timing, P1, maff and noop are not re-run as a fixed term in the confirmatory; see M7).
- *Why.* The confirmatory stop at 43,200 s is hard and burns the seed block. A projection that is biased low at n ≥ 32 is the one place an accounting error costs fresh seeds. After stage 5 the pilot has measured whole-child charges per anchor for a superset of the §10 arms, which is an upper bound it does not use.
- *Fix.* In `confirmatory_count`, publish a second projection: n × (max over development anchors of the stage-5 per-anchor `modules` + `retune` charges plus that anchor's train, noop, maff and tcool charges) + a fixed term. Take the larger of the two for `within_ceiling`, and carry both into `admit_launch`.

**I4. The V0 "zero module bitwise identity on every family" clause is checked for one module form only** (`m0/pilot.py:420`, `:615`; `fit.py:146` `identity_check`).
- *What.* `zero_module_identity: True` rests on `identity_check` inside `materiality` (R18c), which tests only the two-tensor nonlinear `zero_adapter`. The affine two-tensor, three-tensor (M-exp) and four-tensor (h = 64, h = 8) zero adapters are never checked, on CPU or on card. `spreads()` passes the literal `True` into `v0()`.
- *Why.* §3: "At zero weights every module is bitwise identity with the host." §7 V0 names "every family". With P2 and P3 retired, the M0 reading is every module form. The clause is published as passed on narrower evidence than its text, and no ruling records the narrower reading.
- *Fix.* Add a cheap check, in the `noop` child or the acceptance matrix: for each `adapter_shapes` kind, a zero adapter's `losses(host, dev, adapter)` must equal `losses(host, dev, None)` bitwise. Publish the flags and feed them into `v0()`. Otherwise record a ruling that "family" means the P1 impairment family.

**I5. No test feeds a real worker receipt through the supervisor's readers** (`tests/test_m0_worker.py:450-477`; `tests/test_m0_pilot.py` `FakeCluster`).
- *What.* `test_m0_worker` hand-builds an `AnchorResult` from real child output. `test_m0_pilot` drives `receipts.anchor` only with `FakeCluster` receipts written to mirror the worker's keys. The `receipt → source → anchor → quantities` path, including `inventory()` on real `Candidate.report()` output, `printed_counts` from real `retained_parameters`, and `complement_unchanged` from a real retune child, is never exercised end to end.
- *Why.* By inspection the keys match today. A future drift would surface only at stage-5 aggregation on GPU, after about 10,000 s of spend.
- *Fix.* One CPU test: run the real `noop`, `tcool`, `static`, `modules` and `retune` children on the existing 64-image fixture (as `test_modules_runs_every_module_arm…` already does), then call `receipts.source(...)` and `receipts.anchor(seed, sources, PILOT_ARMS)` and `stats.quantities`, and assert `printed_counts`/`complement_unchanged` are True and the harm labels parse.
