## Findings

1. **MAJOR — draft §6 (N_eq, static contrast, T-more) and §7 "read alongside".** Claim: these three readings compare points on the (retained parameters, loss) plane. Evidence: §1 names the confound itself ("the module was fitted on 2,000 examples the host never saw"), and §4 confines the 2,000 conditioning examples to module and re-tune arms. S-static, T-more and every F(s) host see the 40,000-example partition only; M-sq, R-272, R-all see 42,000. D1 and the re-tune fraction are data-matched; the static contrast, G(T-more) vs G(M-sq), and N_eq are not. In the repair framing extra data was irrelevant; in the capacity framing "parameters versus data" is the whole question, and N_eq is the only quantity in the draft that states efficiency in John's units. On any plausible frontier slope (~0.1 nats per doubling), 272 parameters of width buys ~1e-4 nats, so N_eq will report that 272 parameters "bought" tens of thousands of width parameters — a number driven by the 2,000 fresh examples and the undertrained recipe, not by the module. Change: give every host-level point the same R-all treatment on the 2,000 examples before it enters N_eq, the static contrast or T-more (S-static+R-all, F(s)+R-all), or label all three "unmatched data, descriptive" and remove them from the §7 programme-reading sentences. If the matched version is too expensive, drop the 0.75 and 1.5 widths from M0 (saves ~650 s) and keep only the 0.5 host, which the fallback path needs.

2. **MAJOR — draft §5 R-272 / R-aff, §6 D1, §7 V1.** Claim: V1 "present" means new parameters beat re-tuning an equal count. Evidence: the verdict comparator R-272 is 272 scattered entries of one conv drawn at random — the weakest zero-added route one could name. R-aff (170 BN-affine and bias entries, zero added, the classic strong fine-tune route) is "descriptive" only. STEERING rule 2 says efficiency is "a comparison against named cheaper routes", plural. As written V1 can read "present" while R-aff ≥ G(M-sq). Change: D1 = G(M-sq) − max(G(R-272), G(R-aff)), or make R-aff a bold verdict arm; consider choosing the 272-entry subset by gradient magnitude rather than a fixed-seed draw so the comparator is not a straw man.

3. **MAJOR — draft §4 "Refit after truncation", §6 D2, §7 V2.** Claim: D2 is the Liu et al. counter-control ("the cut-down module retrained from scratch is the same arm as built small"). Evidence: the verdict arm M-wide→8 receives M-wide's full 18-candidate grid plus 800 refit steps; M-small receives only its own grid. Liu et al.'s control requires a matched training budget; CONCEPT asks for "targeted controls for extra steps". Rule 4 books the compute but booking does not match it. Change: either read V2 on the as-sliced module (the draft's own "strict reading"), or give M-small matched total optimiser steps to M-wide + refit and state it.

4. **MAJOR — STEERING §5 ladder gating rule and §6 gate reinterpretation; question (a).** Claim: the ladder leads to CONCEPT's objective. Evidence: (i) "A rung opens only when the previous rung's verdict says present" puts M2 (grow-and-remove on a starved host — CONCEPT's "central bet") behind M1 (the generated builder — D09: "a candidate, not mandatory"). An M1 negative halts the programme before the central bet is tested; this inverts CONCEPT's priority. (ii) CONCEPT: removal "is not defined as only terminal compression"; V2 is terminal truncation (ExpandNets). §6 then reads V2 as Gate 5 "benefit after removal", which launders a terminal-compression result into the developmental-removal gate. (iii) D02 says John wants a near-empty start; M2/M3 start from the 0.5 "starved" host (85,274 parameters, 30 percent of the reference), which is roughly the 25–40 percent starting model D02 rejected. Nothing in either document says this relaxes D02. Change: make M2 independent of M1's verdict (M1 only decides which builder M2 uses; an SGD builder is admissible); read V2 as the truncation precedent and leave Gate 5 to M2; add the D02 relaxation to §12 as John's decision or add a rung that starts nearer empty.

5. **MAJOR — STEERING §3 and draft §1; question (b).** Claim: attempt 4 contains "the first reading on the real objective … lowered development loss on every host it was fitted to, whether or not it could see any 'lost' information", "by 0.04 to 0.24 nats". Evidence from receipts: every host the module was fitted to was impaired (P1 attenuated post hoc, P5/P6 bottleneck-trained). P8 (intact host, no impairment — exactly the M0 M-sq arm) was never opened; no "P8" appears in the attempt-4 receipts. The P5/P6 gain range is 0.037–0.260 nats (not 0.24), and the P1 fits gained 0.50–0.81 vs their impaired no-op, so "every host … 0.04 to 0.24" is inconsistent. The hold's "improves an intact host by about 0.1 to 0.2 nats on P1" is an inference from B3 landing below the A1-restored intact loss; the per-anchor margins are 0.048, 0.049, 0.126, 0.137, 0.142, 0.207, and the module that achieved them reads the attenuated tensor, which is not the M-sq family on z. The sizing prior and X inherit from this. Change: say "every impaired host"; cite the hold table rather than a range; state that the unimpaired-host fit is unmeasured and that M-sq is that measurement. Also correct draft §4 "certified 1 of 24 fits": the receipt certifies anchor 3003 at both P5 and P6 at w4 (2 of 24 bottleneck fits; 0 of 6 P1 fits), i.e. 2 of 30.

6. **MAJOR — STEERING §4 rule 1 and draft §12; questions (c) and the primary-endpoint question.** Claim: loss is primary, accuracy reported beside. Evidence: John said "accuracy" in both sentences; CONCEPT's uncommitted diff attributes "accuracy gained per parameter retained" to him, but "per" and "retained" are the author's formalisation. Choosing loss is a defensible proxy (it is what every arm optimises and what the paired bootstrap measures) but it is an interpretation of the objective's unit and is absent from the J-table. Change: add it as J0 with the reason, add an accuracy guard to V1 (dev accuracy not below no-op beyond noise), and attribute the formalisation to the author in CONCEPT.md.

Omitted decisions that are John's: which host is primary (reference versus starved — CONCEPT starts near-empty, §1 already concedes the reference host "is not where capacity is missing" may be the answer, and the 0.5 pair costs 1,200 s); loss as the primary unit (item 6); which zero-added route defines "efficient" (item 2); D2 strict versus refit (item 3); the D02 relaxation (item 4); retirement of the published P2/P3/P5/P6 families and the former arms (§2 does this by fiat); and that X = 0.02 is inherited from the repair margin ("a worthwhile repair margin", v4 §7, D15) rather than derived for capacity. Change: move J5/J8 to the implementation plan; add J0 unit, J-host, J-route, J-D2, J-D02, J-retire; present X with a capacity justification (for example against the frontier slope in nats per log₂ parameter) or label it inherited.

## Verdict

V1 measures a defensible precondition — site capacity that ordinary SGD finds and re-tuning does not — and the draft says so; it is not a repair question, and the P1 known answer is correctly confined to instrument validation. But it is not yet a measurement of "accuracy at the most efficient parameter cost": the one quantity that states efficiency in John's units (N_eq) and the two cheaper-route readings (static, more training) are confounded by the 2,000 fresh examples the draft itself names; the V1 comparator is the weakest zero-added route; the co-primary V2 is not budget-matched to the control it cites; and the unit (loss, not accuracy) was chosen by the author without surfacing it to John. The ladder reaches CONCEPT's half-parameters claim in form but gates the central add-and-remove bet behind the optional builder, maps a terminal-truncation result onto the developmental-removal gate, and silently starts from the 30 percent host D02 rejected. The attempt-4 narrative overstates what was measured (no unimpaired-host fit exists) and has two wrong numbers. Nothing here is a BLOCKER because the documents authorise nothing and every defect is fixable in a v2 without new evidence; the majors (items 1–7) should be resolved and the omitted decisions put to John before any implementation plan is written.
