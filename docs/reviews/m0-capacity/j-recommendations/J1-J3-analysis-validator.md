## Power formula check

The report's n* is not a power formula and does not claim to be: it is the code's half-width rule (one-sided 95 %, z = 1.645, v4 inflation 1.25, half-width ≤ X/2), `contracts.py:185`, restated in draft §9 step 6. The 80 % figure enters only in the MDE column, (1.645 + 0.8416)·1.25·SD/√n, which is the correct one-sided α = 0.05, 80 %-power, paired one-sample z formula (with the 1.25 inflation carried over), and in the exact sign-test power. For contrast, a true power sizing to detect X = 0.02 against 0 at ρ = 0.9 needs n = 18, below the 32 floor; at ρ = 0 it needs 180, not 316. The two criteria disagree about what "sized at 32" means, so the report's labelling of X as "smallest margin resolvable at n = 32" is correct only in the half-width sense it states (indeterminate band X ± hw), not in a detection-power sense.
