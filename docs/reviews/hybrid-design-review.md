# Independent hybrid design review

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: design/statistics review only; no code changes or
training. Reviewed `HYBRID_CONTRACT.md`, the implementation plan, the emmy
design and existing ridge instrument, and the independently preserved Claude
design and Emrakul/Tamiyo revision.

## Initial assessment

Recommended two separately interpretable assays: learned formation on held-out
procedural lineages within a disclosed shared nonlinear family, and planted
same-feature learning/taper/physical-removal mechanics. Required explicit
gradient-information disclosure for paired moments, raw and admitted outcomes,
retrieval/shuffle/cheap-SGD controls, early static capacity, actual parameter
deletion, and no population safety or special growth claim. Probe identification
belongs in a separate ambiguity fixture; identity-host probes need not improve
formation. Shared template families must not be renamed by seed to imply
unseen-family transfer.

## Final assessment

**Approved for the bounded implementation. No design blockers remain.**

The saved contract incorporates these recommendations, fixes the 32/8/8
lineage and 64/24/64 example counts, fixes the lifecycle final MSE target at
0.01 before evaluation, includes a static comparator with matching final size,
and distinguishes software acceptance from measured scientific outcomes.
Source designs remain separate. This approval does not certify implementation
correctness or predict that demonstration targets will pass.

## Confidence assessment

High confidence that the written contract bounds the intended claims and
contains the necessary controls, based on direct inspection. Numerical success
and resource compliance remain unmeasured.

## Risk assessment

Low implementation-scope risk; medium interpretation risk; changes are readily
reversible. The contract addresses the main interpretation risks explicitly:
shared family/basis privilege, gradient-bearing evidence, algebraically
foldable same-feature lifecycle capacity, and temporary versus final size.

## Information gaps

Implementation, numerical conditioning, measured runtime and test outcomes do
not exist yet. Exact basis definitions, hyperparameters, admission threshold
and lifecycle schedules are assigned to the implementers and must be recorded
before inspecting final evaluation outcomes.

## Caveats and required follow-ups

Perform the planned independent code/result review. Verify isolation, cost
accounting, actual deletion and reporting of failed targets. In particular,
continued full-host training may produce residuals outside the generator's
four-dimensional training family; treat the resulting lifecycle behavior as a
measured limitation rather than silently projecting targets or retuning on
evaluation examples. No additional pre-implementation review is required.
