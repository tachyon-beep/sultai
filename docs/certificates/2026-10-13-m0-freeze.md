## What is frozen

Every value in the preregistration marked [taken] or [proposed], the arm
sets, the overrun and stop rules, the seed blocks in §11 and Appendix A, and
the decisions J0–J10 in §12. Values marked [measured] are Gate 3 attempt-4
receipts and were already fixed; values marked [pilot-fill] are filled by
the pilot under §9 and are the only fields the pilot may write.

The window in which John could reverse an author's resolution in §12 in one
line (J0 in particular: loss the sole primary unit, accuracy a labelled
secondary that conditions no verdict) closed at this freeze without a
reversal. Any later change to any of the three pinned files is a new
version or a dated amendment with its own hash, and changes the composed
identity: the preregistration becomes v4; the gate-ladder amendment becomes
amendment 2; a change to STEERING.md is a dated steering amendment. A
receipt carrying the identity above refers to exactly these bytes.
