## The defect

§7 (line 429) promises that "D2s, the random-rank contrasts, the survival
curves and the extended arms are reported beside with bounds and no
verdict role", and §6 (lines 366-368) promises, across anchors, "median
with one-sided 95 percent percentile anchor-bootstrap bounds (v4 §7), and
an exact sign-test bound beside it at confirmatory n". The §10 confirmatory
arm set [J7] stops at M-wide→8, so the survival curve
G(M-wide→h)/G(M-wide), h ∈ {16, 8, 4, 2}, could only ever be read on the
six development anchors, where the 500th and 9,500th order statistics of
the median bootstrap sit near the sample minimum and maximum. The
implementation certified at gate B (commit `978850a`) also bounded only
D1, D1′, D2, D3, ΔAcc (and D5 under the 14-hour flag) at confirmatory n,
attached the sign test to D1 alone, and returned the whole survival curve
undefined whenever any width was absent.

## A3.1 Survival-curve widths join the confirmatory set (§10, lines 558-561)

The confirmatory arm set is extended by **M-wide→16, M-wide→4 and
M-wide→2, each as sliced with fold and as refit** (one 800-step refit at
the M-wide selected rate, §5 line 292), so that the survival curves
G(M-wide→h)/G(M-wide), h ∈ {16, 8, 4, 2}, sliced and refit, are reported at
confirmatory n with medians and bounds as §7 promises. They have no
verdict role. The sliced cuts are inside the per-anchor cuts, folds and
evaluations line already booked (§8); each refit is one single-rate
800-step run, booked at the pilot-measured `refit-single` kernel (§8
nominal: 3.2 s per 800 steps plus one development evaluation, about 10 s
per anchor, about 320 s at n = 32 against the 43,200 s ceiling). The
stage 6 projection (§9) and the confirmatory launcher carry the arms
automatically because both read the arm set.

## A3.2 Bounds and the sign test beside every reported quantity (§6, lines 366-368)

At confirmatory n, every §6 quantity the confirmatory arm set defines on
every anchor is published as its median with one-sided 95 percent
percentile anchor-bootstrap bounds and the exact sign-test bound beside it:
D1, D1′, D2, D2s, the random-rank D2 and D2s, D3, G(T-cool), r_aff, ΔAcc,
G(R-temp), G(R-cal) and every survival ratio (each width, sliced and
refit); D5 with and without the refit when S-static is in the set. The
verdict logic is unchanged: only D1 (V1), D2 (V2), D3, ΔAcc and D1′
(labels) and D5 (the static reading) condition anything; the rest are
reported beside with no verdict role. A quantity undefined on an anchor
that no verdict reads is published as undefined with the anchor named,
and does not stop the reading; a verdict quantity undefined on an anchor
holds the reading as before. A survival curve reports every width that
ran and names the absent widths; it is undefined only when M-wide is
absent or G(M-wide) ≤ 0. The pilot's development medians (§9 stage 7)
include the survival ratios, for the record only.

## A3.3 S-static has no no-op rule of its own (gate-B decision 2, recorded)

The preregistration gives T-cool its own no-op rule (§6, D3) and is silent
for S-static. The reading implemented and certified at gate B stands: D5
compares L(host + M-sq), after the anchor's own no-op rule, against the
static host with its designed-in module (raw, and after the module grid
refit), with no no-op rule applied to the static host. S-static remains
descriptive at the pilot and reads only under the 14-hour flag (§7, §10).
