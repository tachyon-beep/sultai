## The asymmetry

§4's no-op rule (N0: the raw trained host against its BatchNorm-recalibrated
twin R-bn, the lower mean selection loss chosen, ties to raw) is applied to
the anchor host before every module fit, and §6 gives the finished T-cool
host "its own N0 rule" before D3. The preregistration is silent for
S-static, so D5 = L(host + M-sq) − L(S-static + refit) compared a route
that had the no-op rule against a static host that did not. The
recalibration is worth about one percent of a module gain on the anchor
hosts; left asymmetric it could tilt D5 toward insertion for a reason that
has nothing to do with design-in.

## A4.1 S-static after its own no-op rule (§5 line 295, §6 line 345)

After the ten-epoch training with the module present from step zero, the
**complete static model** (inner host with its designed-in module) is given
the §4 no-op rule: its BatchNorm statistics are re-estimated over the same
2,000 fit examples by the same cumulative pass, **with the module in the
forward** so that every statistic downstream of the site is that of the
designed-in network; the raw and recalibrated static models are compared by
mean selection loss, each on features recomputed from itself; the lower
wins and a tie keeps the raw model. The chosen model is the frozen static
host every later reading uses: the no-refit term L(S-static), the module
grid refit warm-started from its learned module (S-static:refit), and D5
with and without the refit. The choice ("R-bn" or "raw") and both state
hashes are published in the static host's receipt and in the pilot's host
receipt beside T-cool's.

Unchanged: the static host's training recipe, seed, initialisation and
data stream (§5), the V0 clause that its tensors are bitwise the anchor's
at step 0 (§7, acceptance item (iii)), the warm-start refit, S-static's
descriptive role at the pilot, its SD(D5) admission rule and the 14-hour
ceiling (§7, §10). The pass costs one recalibration and two selection
evaluations inside the static child, measured by the pilot as the whole
child (§8 "S-static hosts" line, ruling R23).
