## TCD means technical concept demonstrator

initial documentation commit preserved the ambiguity rather than guessing.

near-empty, physical removal, or the final parameter-efficiency target.

## Agreed intent

1. Addition and deliberate physical removal jointly drive development.
2. Start near-empty; compare equal capability at about half final retained
   parameters. Search/training cost is a separate accounting dimension.
3. Tamiyo provides coarse site/intervention/budget observations. The seed
   obtains rich paired telemetry itself and can probe a frozen shadow host.
   Tamiyo need not diagnose the entire defect.
4. Diffusion is a candidate mechanism, not a requirement or a synonym for
   pruning. Denoising alone does not remove physical parameters.
5. Temporary structure may shape learning, hand over useful function, and
   disappear. Lack of instant gain alone must not reject every candidate.

## Bounded first probe

One known 16-channel site; one nonlinear 272-parameter adapter; a frozen
synthetic shadow host; explicit information and evaluation boundaries. Hold
the Tamiyo instruction fixed across coarse, paired, and paired-plus-probe
conditions. Test local repair formation only. Full model growth/removal,
production host integration, trained generators, and half-parameter claims
are later milestones requiring additional design and experiments.
